# Round 3 Plan & Long-Term Roadmap

Future roadmap for BrandLens moving into Round 3 and production readiness:

## 1. Enterprise Multi-Tenancy & Workspace RBAC
- Role-based access control with SSO (Okta, Google Workspace, Azure AD).
- Team workspaces with granular permissions (Junior Copywriter, Senior Brand Lead, Legal Counsel, External Agency).

## 2. Omnichannel Ingestion & CMS Integrations
- Figma Plugin: Live brand governance inside Figma design frames.
- Google Docs / Word Add-in: Real-time copy audit while authors write drafts.
- Social Media CMS integrations (HubSpot, Hootsuite, WordPress).

## 3. Advanced Fine-Tuned Domain Models
- Custom LoRA adapters fine-tuned on company historical marketing materials and legal clearances.
- Multi-lingual brand tone alignment across 15+ regional and international languages.

## 4. Automated Workflow Escalation & Webhooks
- Automated Slack / Microsoft Teams webhook alerts when high-severity legal claims are submitted for review.
- Automated compliance audit trails exportable for ISO / GDPR verification.
